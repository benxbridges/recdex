## Pitch 1: the de-clutter + cook mode angle

**Subject:** A free website that turns any recipe URL into distraction-free cook mode

Hi [Name],

I noticed your piece on [recent relevant article — e.g. recipe-site bloat,
cooking apps, kitchen tech]. I built something your readers might like.

RecDex (recipeindex.org) is a free website — no app, no signup — that takes
any recipe URL and turns it into a step-by-step "cook mode": one step per
screen, big type, and timers that are automatically detected from the recipe
text ("simmer 10–15 minutes", "4 minutes per side") and actually ring, even
with the phone locked.

It also works from a photo of a cookbook page.

The angle for your readers: everyone's saved-recipes folder is a graveyard.
This closes the gap between finding a recipe and standing at the stove with
it — without downloading another $60/year subscription app (ReciMe) or being
locked to iOS (Pestle, Crouton, Mela).

Happy to share a demo video, or paste-this-URL examples that show extraction
working on messy real-world pages. No PR firm, just the builder.

[Ben]
