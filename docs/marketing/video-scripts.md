# Short-Form Video Scripts (TikTok / Reels)

Format: faceless, hands + phone + food. 20–40 seconds. Hook in the first 1.5s.
Cadence: 3/week. Ride recipes that are already trending — search TikTok's
"recipe" sounds and the /trending page data we already scrape.

---

## Script 1 — "POV: you actually cooked the recipe you saved"

**Hook (text on screen):** "You've saved 200 recipes. Cooked: 3."
1. Screen-record scrolling a saved folder of viral recipes (relatable shame)
2. Copy one link → paste into recipeindex.org → "Cook now"
3. Cut to real kitchen: phone propped up, one big step on screen
4. Timer rings (show phone locked → notification fires) — *this is the money shot*
5. Finished dish + text: "Free site, no app. Link in bio."

**Caption:** the difference between saving recipes and cooking them is one paste 🍳

---

## Script 2 — "Recipe sites vs. what I actually need"

**Hook:** "Why is the recipe at the bottom of a novel?"
1. Screen-record a real recipe page: scroll… scroll… ad… scroll… (speed ramp)
2. Hard cut: same URL pasted into RecDex → clean recipe appears in ~3s
3. "Cook now" → step one, huge type
4. Wet-hands tap with a knuckle to advance the step
5. Text: "recipeindex.org — free, no ads, no life story"

**Caption:** no shade to food bloggers — but at the stove I just need the steps

---

## Script 3 — "Cook a TikTok recipe without leaving your kitchen"

**Hook:** "That pasta you just saved? Cook it tonight."
1. Show a trending recipe video (duet/stitch where allowed)
2. Copy link → paste into RecDex → extracted ingredients appear
3. Quick pan: shopping list checked off
4. Cook mode steps + timer firing on locked phone
5. Plate it. Text: "Found → Cooked. recipeindex.org"

**Caption:** stop letting your saved folder be a graveyard

---

## Script 4 — "Cookbook page → guided cooking" (scan feature beat)

**Hook:** "Your cookbooks have a cook mode now."
1. Open a real cookbook to a dense page
2. Snap photo in RecDex scan → structured recipe appears
3. Cook mode, timers detected from the text ("simmer 20 min" highlighted)
4. Side-by-side: open book getting splattered vs. phone with one clean step
5. Text: "Scan any cookbook page — free at recipeindex.org"

**Caption:** the cookbook stays clean, you still get timers

---

## Production notes

- Always show the timer firing on a **locked phone** — it's the single most
  differentiated moment we have, and no recipe-manager competitor can show it
- End cards always say the URL out loud in text: **recipeindex.org** (web-first
  is the moat — "no app" is a feature, say it every time)
- Reuse trending audio; keep cuts under 1.5s each
- Never claim a recipe is ours — credit the creator on screen when stitching
